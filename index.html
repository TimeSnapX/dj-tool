DJ TOOL  |  2-CHANNEL ALL-IN-ONE
SOURCE
How it works / limits
  • Three kinds of source per deck: a YouTube / YouTube Music link, a Spotify link, or a local audio file (mp3, wav, m4a, ogg, flac) from your own device. Load a file with the deck's LOAD FILE button, the LOAD 1 / LOAD 2 buttons beside the screen, or by dragging it onto the deck or screen.
  • Local files get full control. They are decoded in your browser with the Web Audio API and never leave your device (no upload). Real on local decks: play/pause, cue (set / return / hold-to-preview), jog scratch and pitch-bend, tempo fader (changes pitch too, like vinyl), loop in / out / reloop / ½× / 2×, 8 hot-cue pads (tap to set, tap to jump, Shift + click to delete; saved per file name + size), TRIM, LOW / MID / HI EQ, COLOR filter, channel fader, level meters, a real waveform from the decoded audio, and BEAT FX (echo, flanger, reverb) with TIME, LEVEL/DEPTH, ON/OFF and channel select.
  • YouTube & Spotify decks are limited by the official players. Their audio stays inside the embeds, so there is no EQ, COLOR, TRIM, effects, scratching or level meter for them (those knobs dim when such a deck is loaded). YouTube decks do get play/pause, cue, seek, channel-fader volume, tempo (the speeds YouTube allows, 0.25×–2×, pitch preserved), repeat, and pads 1–4 as jump-to-0/25/50/75%. Spotify decks get play/pause, cue, seek and repeat only.
  • Crossfader and MASTER level apply to both types: equal-power curve; Spotify embeds can't be faded, so the crossfader and Auto-mix hand them off (start / stop) instead.
  • No beat-matching or BPM: there is no BPM detection or SYNC, so those buttons are decorative. The waveform grid, KEY, QUANTIZE, SLIP, MIC, AUX, USB, headphone CUE / MIXING / LEVEL, BOOTH, SOUND COLOR FX buttons and the remaining pad modes are decorative too (tooltip: "Not available in the web version").
  • Official players only: nothing from YouTube or Spotify is downloaded, recorded or modified, and no API keys or accounts are needed. Spotify full tracks need you to be logged in to Spotify in this browser, otherwise the embed plays 30-second previews.
  • Some videos can't be embedded (the owner disabled it). Playlist "Mix" / radio lists aren't supported by the player API. A watch link that also has &list= loads just that video; use a /playlist?list= link for a whole playlist.
  • Autoplay rules: browsers may block sound until you've clicked on the page once.
  • Saved in your browser: queue, history, last streaming link per deck, knob / fader positions and hot cues are stored in localStorage on this device only. Local files themselves can't be remembered (the browser doesn't allow it), so load them again after a refresh.

Keys: Q play/pause deck 1 · P play/pause deck 2 · ←/→ nudge crossfader · M auto-mix · double-click a knob or fader to reset it
